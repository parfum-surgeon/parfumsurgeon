Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Parfum Surgeon?

Parfum Surgeon is a Pakistani fragrance house based in Lahore offering ten signature 50ml perfumes crafted for men, women, and unisex wear.

Do you deliver nationwide across Pakistan?

Yes, we deliver nationwide across Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, and all major cities in Pakistan.

What payment methods do you accept?

You can check out using Cash on Delivery (COD) or direct Meezan Bank transfer.

What bottle size are the fragrances?

Each signature scent comes in a refined 50ml black-and-gold bottle tailored for daily wear and evening occasions.

What is your return and exchange policy?

We offer a 30-day return window for eligible unopened items. Simply email parfumsurgeon@gmail.com with your order reference.
