Return & Exchange Policy

Not completely satisfied with your fragrance? Parfum Surgeon offers a 30-day return policy. If you don't like your purchase, you can request a return within 30 days.

How to Request a Return

1. Email us at parfumsurgeon@gmail.com within 30 days of receiving your order, including your order reference number and the reason for the return.

2. Our team will respond with the next steps for your specific order.

3. Once your return is approved and processed, any applicable refund or exchange will be arranged.

Detailed conditions (such as packaging requirements, who covers return shipping, and refund timing) will be confirmed with you directly when you submit a return request.
