Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Parfum Surgeon collects when you shop with us, and how it is used.

Information We Collect

When you place an order, we collect your name, mobile number, delivery address, and optionally your email address and order notes — only what's needed to deliver your order and contact you about it.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to process and deliver your order, contact you about order status, and respond to support requests. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Order details are stored securely with our backend service provider (Supabase) rather than in your browser. Your cart contents (which products you've added) are stored only in your own browser's local storage and are never transmitted until you submit an order.

Payment Information

For Cash on Delivery, no payment details are collected online. For bank transfer, we ask for a payment reference and, optionally, a receipt image to confirm your payment — we do not collect or store your banking credentials.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to parfumsurgeon@gmail.com.
